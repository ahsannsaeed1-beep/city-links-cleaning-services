# City Links Cleaning Services — website concept

A premium single-page website concept for **City Links Cleaning Services**,
a residential & commercial cleaning business specialising in home and office
cleaning. Built as a VVIP design concept in white, deep navy and emerald with
champagne/silver highlights.

## What's on the site

- Full-screen hero — "Experience the Difference of a Spotless Space."
- Five services: Home, Office, Deep, Regular and One-Off Cleaning, each with
  a detail modal and a quote button that pre-selects that service
- About, Why Choose Us (6 points), and a four-step How It Works timeline
- Masonry gallery with lightbox and a testimonial carousel
- Quote request form and contact form
- Multi-column footer with Privacy Policy / Terms & Conditions

## Honest notes

- **Placeholders:** contact details, location, opening hours and social links
  are shown as "To be confirmed" — none were supplied. Testimonials are
  clearly labelled sample placeholders; no statistics are shown, as no genuine
  figures were supplied.
- **Forms are demo-only:** the quote and contact forms validate and show a
  success message, but nothing is sent or booked.
- **Copy is in Canadian English** (the client is Canadian); no Canadian city
  or address has been invented.
- Photography is real professional cleaning photography; no AI faces.
